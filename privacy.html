JewCal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-11

Summary

JewCal is a native iPhone and Android Hebrew-calendar, zmanim, and reminder app. Everyday features work without internet, and calendars can update automatically when you are connected. JewCal has no accounts, advertising, usage tracking, crash reporting, or billing. Personal calendars, settings, and saved location stay on your device. The internet connections JewCal makes are explained below.

Data kept on your device

JewCal stores the information needed to provide its features in the app's local storage:

  • calendar events, calendars, and reminder choices that you create or import;
  • display, language, diaspora, and notification settings;
  • an optional saved place name, coordinates, and time zone used for zmanim and reminders.

This information is used only by JewCal on your device. We do not receive a copy of it.

Supporter Yahrzeits updates

JewCal includes a copy of Supporter Yahrzeits inside the app, so that calendar is available without internet. While Supporter Yahrzeits is turned on, JewCal checks for an updated copy when the app starts or when the phone allows a background check. These checks normally happen no more than once every six hours. Turning the calendar on starts a check right away. Turning it off stops future Supporter Yahrzeits checks.

When JewCal checks, GitHub Pages can see the address your phone uses on the internet (called an IP address), when it checked, which calendar file it asked for, information needed to connect safely, and the app name JewCal/1. JewCal sends no personal calendar data, saved location, settings, account number, cookies, usage-tracking ID, or advertising ID.

The update file is currently kept on JewCal.org through GitHub Pages. GitHub runs that service and may keep connection records, including IP addresses, for security. Details are in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Calendars added from other websites

If you add a calendar using a web address, JewCal checks that address right away and then about every six hours. Checks continue while the calendar is hidden; hiding it only hides its entries. The website you chose, and any website it sends JewCal to, can see the same information about the check listed above. Each website handles its connection records under its own privacy rules.

An optional calendar password is stored securely on your device and used there to open the downloaded file. JewCal never sends the password to the website. Downloaded calendar contents are stored in JewCal on your device. JewCal does not send the website your personal calendars, saved location, settings, account number, cookies, usage-tracking ID, or advertising ID.

Location

Location is optional. You may select a city from JewCal's bundled offline catalog, or grant location permission after you choose Automatic. JewCal uses the selected location to calculate zmanim, candle-lighting times, and reminders.

Manual city searches, time-zone lookup, and nearest-city matching happen entirely on your device; search text is not sent anywhere. Automatic location uses a one-time request through the phone's location service while JewCal is open. On Android, Google Play services may help provide the location. On iPhone, Apple provides it; if JewCal has no nearby city in its built-in list, Apple's place-name service may also find the place name and time zone. JewCal does not ask for background location or send your saved location during calendar update checks.

Notifications and alarms

JewCal requests notification permission only to show reminders you enable. It uses Android alarms or iOS local notifications to schedule event, holiday, prayer, candle-lighting, and Jewish-day reminders. Notification text is generated on your device.

Import, export, backup, and sharing

JewCal reads calendar files only when you select a file to import. It creates or shares an export only when you choose that action. An exported calendar can contain event titles, dates, descriptions, and reminder details. Once you save or share an export, the destination app, service, or recipient handles that copy under its own practices.

A full JewCal backup contains app settings, the saved location name, coordinates and time zone, calendars, events, and reminder choices. JewCal creates or restores a backup only after you explicitly choose the file. Backup files are not sent to JewCal. You control where they are stored and who can access them.

Data sharing

JewCal does not sell, rent, or share your personal calendar data for advertising, profiling, or usage tracking. Outside services are limited to the calendar websites and optional Apple or Google location services explained above.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it in JewCal, clear JewCal's storage where the phone provides that option, or uninstall the app. Removing an online calendar also deletes the password saved for it. Calendar and backup files that you saved or shared must be deleted from the place where you put them. JewCal keeps no account or uploaded copy of your personal calendars, location, or settings.

GitHub and websites chosen for online calendars control how long they keep connection records and how those records can be deleted. See each website's privacy information for details.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when JewCal's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
